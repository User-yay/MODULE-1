Our Story

The foundation of our craft dates back to 1892 in the historic quarter of Naples, Italy, where the Marone family first mastered the art of the wood-fired hearth. Decades later, in 1924, young Antonio Marone carried that timeless tradition across the Atlantic, preserving a precious heirloom sourdough culture through the gates of Ellis Island. In the winter of 1931, amidst the rugged brick and cobblestone of New York’s working-class waterfront, Antonio established our first brick oven. He refused the modern shortcuts of the era, relying solely on hardwood fires, hand-crushed plum tomatoes, and patience. Nearly a century later, we stand on the same foundation. Every pie we bake honors the original 1931 recipe, preserving the authentic, historic taste of old-world tradition.
