Ride Dictionary

Sky Coaster
A fast ride that takes visitors high above the park. almost like a sight seeing tour, but really fun!
Treasure Trail
An escape room-like experience where visitors solve puzzles to find hidden treasure.
River Rush
A water ride that moves quickly through twists and turns. Basically a natural water slide!(class 3-4 rapids)

Safety Mission

Please report to Gate 3 before the challenge begins.

The rescue drill will happen near River Zone.

Ride Commands

Start the coaster: ride.start("Sky Coaster")

Open the trail: trail.open("Treasure Trail")

Stop the ride: ride.stop("River Rush")

Park Short Forms
